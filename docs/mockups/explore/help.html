Common questions

Which sites work?

YouTube and many other video sites. A few sites won’t work.

Why would a video need a sign-in?

Private and age-restricted videos can need your account. MediaGrabber uses your browser’s sign-in only if you choose a browser.

Where do my files go?

To your Downloads folder, unless you choose another one.

Is it okay to download anything?

Only download what you have the right to.

Something not working?

Try these in order.

  1. Look at the top of the window

    A check that needs attention is outlined. Click it to see what is wrong and what you can do.

    ConnectionYouTube & sign-inToolsStorage

  2. Run a health check

    Diagnostics tests the app and lets you copy the details.

  3. Report the problem

    Diagnostics opens first, so you can copy the details into your report.
